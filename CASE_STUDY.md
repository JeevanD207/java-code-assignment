# Case Study Scenarios to discuss

## Scenario 1: Cost Allocation and Tracking
**Situation**: The company needs to track and allocate costs accurately across different Warehouses and Stores. The costs include labor, inventory, transportation, and overhead expenses.

**Task**: Discuss the challenges in accurately tracking and allocating costs in a fulfillment environment. Think about what are important considerations for this, what are previous experiences that you have you could related to this problem and elaborate some questions and considerations

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
Tracking costs accurately can be challenging because warehouses and stores have different expenses such as labor, transportation, inventory, and overhead costs. I would first understand how these costs are currently recorded and allocated.

Direct costs can be assigned easily, but shared costs need clear allocation rules. I would also check the accuracy and consistency of data coming from different systems.

Some important questions would be: How are costs currently tracked? How are shared costs allocated? How frequently is the data updated? How is historical cost information maintained?

The main focus would be on having clear business rules and reliable data so that costs can be tracked accurately and used for better decision-making.

## Scenario 2: Cost Optimization Strategies
**Situation**: The company wants to identify and implement cost optimization strategies for its fulfillment operations. The goal is to reduce overall costs without compromising service quality.

**Task**: Discuss potential cost optimization strategies for fulfillment operations and expected outcomes from that. How would you identify, prioritize and implement these strategies?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**

To reduce costs, I would first identify the areas where the company is spending the most, such as labor, transportation, inventory, or warehouse operations. I would use the available cost and operational data to identify areas that can be improved.

The strategies should be prioritized based on cost savings, business impact, and implementation effort. For example, improving transportation routes or reducing unnecessary inventory could help reduce costs without affecting service quality.

I would implement the changes gradually and monitor the results. The main goal would be to reduce costs while maintaining the expected service quality.

## Scenario 3: Integration with Financial Systems
**Situation**: The Cost Control Tool needs to integrate with existing financial systems to ensure accurate and timely cost data. The integration should support real-time data synchronization and reporting.

**Task**: Discuss the importance of integrating the Cost Control Tool with financial systems. What benefits the company would have from that and how would you ensure seamless integration and data synchronization?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**

Integrating the Cost Control Tool with financial systems is important because it helps keep cost information accurate and up to date. It can reduce manual work and provide better visibility into the overall costs.

I would first understand what financial systems are currently being used and what data needs to be shared between the systems. For seamless integration, it is important to have proper data mapping, validation, and error handling.I would also consider how frequently the data needs to be synchronized and how failures or duplicate data should be handled. The main goal would be to ensure reliable data synchronization and accurate reporting for better business decisions.

## Scenario 4: Budgeting and Forecasting
**Situation**: The company needs to develop budgeting and forecasting capabilities for its fulfillment operations. The goal is to predict future costs and allocate resources effectively.

**Task**: Discuss the importance of budgeting and forecasting in fulfillment operations and what would you take into account designing a system to support accurate budgeting and forecasting?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
Budgeting and forecasting are important because they help the company plan future expenses and use resources effectively. I would consider previous cost data, expected order volume, labor requirements, transportation costs, and other operational expenses.

The system should also allow the forecast to be updated when business conditions change. Comparing actual costs with the planned budget would help identify differences early and take necessary action. The main goal would be to support better planning and control costs effectively.

## Scenario 5: Cost Control in Warehouse Replacement
**Situation**: The company is planning to replace an existing Warehouse with a new one. The new Warehouse will reuse the Business Unit Code of the old Warehouse. The old Warehouse will be archived, but its cost history must be preserved.

**Task**: Discuss the cost control aspects of replacing a Warehouse. Why is it important to preserve cost history and how this relates to keeping the new Warehouse operation within budget?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
When replacing a warehouse, preserving the old warehouse's cost history is important for accurate reporting and future analysis. Even if the new warehouse uses the same Business Unit Code, the historical costs should not be mixed with the new warehouse's costs.

I would consider keeping the old warehouse archived while maintaining its historical records separately. This would help compare previous and current costs and understand whether the new warehouse is operating within the planned budget. The main focus would be on preserving accurate historical data while tracking the new warehouse's costs correctly.
